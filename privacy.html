Privacy Policy — Phap29

Effective date: 23 July 2026 · Operated by FINNDEEMEESOOK COMPANY LIMITED, Thailand

1. What this application is

Phap29 is an internal business tool operated by FINNDEEMEESOOK COMPANY LIMITED. It is used solely by our own staff to schedule and publish content (text and photos) to Facebook Pages that we own and administer, through Meta's Pages API. It is not offered to, installed by, or used by the general public.

2. Data we collect

The application does not collect, request, or process any personal data from end users or members of the public. The only Platform Data it handles are the Page identifiers and Page access tokens of our own Facebook Pages, which are required to publish content to those Pages.

3. How data is stored and protected

Page access tokens are stored in a private configuration file on a company-owned computer with access restricted to page administrators. They are never published, transmitted to third parties, or embedded in public code.

4. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer any Platform Data to any third party. No data processors or external service providers have access to Platform Data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept only while they are needed to operate our own Pages and are deleted when no longer required. To request deletion of any data related to this application, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
